Before you start

Twelve elements, three statements each

Short on time? The 5-minute version asks one statement per element and three questions about what exists. It gives a quick view only.

For each element: three statements about how it feels in practice. After each part of the bridge, a short check of what actually exists, only where it is not yet working well. Allow about 30 minutes, including your result. Answer for what really happens, not what is supposed to happen.
